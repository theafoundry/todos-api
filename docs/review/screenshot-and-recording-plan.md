## Genuine listing shots to collect

Use a separately authorized dedicated synthetic reviewer account and the actual
ChatGPT client. Record the source SHA, UTC capture time, client/browser version,
account fixture reset time and selected appearance/display mode separately.

1. Today Plan inline/panel with a realistic two-hour, medium-energy plan,
   legible synthetic titles and available/planned/remaining minutes.
2. Today Plan fullscreen with all actions visible and a coherent task order.
3. Completed synthetic task followed by undo and refreshed authoritative state;
   use a recording or clearly ordered evidence frames, not one ambiguous still.
4. Narrow/mobile layout and dark appearance, with controls readable and no
   clipping. Final listing selection may use fewer shots; retain QA evidence.

Capture the app as it actually appears. Avoid browser developer tools, private
emails, account UUIDs, raw request state, tokens, consent artifacts and password
prompts. Keep captions useful and legible. Review final images at native size
and record dimensions and SHA-256 before adding paths to the public manifest.
