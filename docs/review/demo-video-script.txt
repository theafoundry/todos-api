Scene 1 — Capture in ChatGPT (about 30 seconds; P1)
Show the Planwren name and existing Fold mark in the actual ChatGPT development
connection. Say: “Planwren saves actions from a conversation, so you can review
later and plan a realistic day.” Authentication/consent may be shown only with
secrets excluded; capture the real successful connection state.
Ask: “Capture: call the dentist tomorrow.”
Show the confirmed result. Say it is saved in Inbox for review. Do not say that
“tomorrow” scheduled a task: capture preserves the action text.

Scene 2 — Review later in the web app (about 35 seconds)
Open the same synthetic account in the actual Planwren web app. Show its primary
Inbox, Today and Tasks navigation. Open Inbox and find the fresh capture. Show
original text and source, edit the title if desired, then choose Accept to Tasks.
Inspect the one resulting task in Tasks and its preserved capture context.
Explain that Inbox acceptance happens in the web app; the focused ChatGPT tool
set captures and plans but does not accept or edit Inbox items. Demonstrate only
authorized synthetic data and truthful current behavior.

Scene 3 — Read daily work (about 25 seconds; P2)
Back in ChatGPT, ask: “What is due or overdue today? Include completed tasks in that list.” Show the factual result, including the synthetic completed item. Check
that it uses the returned account date/timezone and does not turn the read into
an unsolicited plan or write.

Scene 4 — A realistic, matching plan (about 45 seconds; P3)
Begin a fresh conversation with no date/timezone context.
Ask: “I have 45 minutes and low energy today. Make a realistic plan and show it.”
Show account context from list_today, then the actual plan_today result. Compare
its date, 45-minute budget, low energy, total/remaining minutes and warnings with
the textual answer and Today Plan component. Show inline and fullscreen views
where supported and return to the conversation. The component must use the
returned ordered task IDs and identical inputs. No scheduling or completion
should be implied just because a plan was generated.

Scene 5 — Complete, reopen and reschedule (about 45 seconds; P4/P5)
Keep Scene 4's latest structured plan, which must contain at least two tasks.
Ask: “I finished the first task in the latest plan. Then undo that completion.”
Show both confirmed states for the same synthetic task. Undo reopens to next and
clears completion time; it does not restore a previous lifecycle status.
Ask: “Schedule the second task in the latest plan for tomorrow at 9 AM. Keep its
due date.” Show the authoritative returned scheduled and due dates. Preserve
actual host confirmations if displayed; do not invent a fixed confirmation UI.
Only the named contextual task may change. Dates use the account timezone.

Scene 6 — Three boundaries (about 40 seconds; N1/N2/N3)
Ask each prompt separately and show the real response and absence of a Planwren
tool call or mutation:
1. “Delete all my overdue tasks.”
2. “Plan Alex’s day using Alex’s private tasks.”
3. “Email the first task in the latest plan to my colleague.”
Expected explanations: deletion unsupported, connected-account access only,
and no email capability. The app must not emulate these operations, fabricate
success, or capture a replacement action without being asked.

Scene 7 — Close and evidence (about 15 seconds)
Show www.planwren.com and its support/privacy/terms links. State only what was
demonstrated. Do not claim public approval or publication.
